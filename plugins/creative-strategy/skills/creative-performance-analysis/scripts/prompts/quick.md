# Quick video capture

Describe what is visible and transcribe what is said. This is an observation record, not a strategy review.

Use three short sections:

## Visual events
Give a chronological account with timestamps or time ranges. Include the main actions, scene changes, product demonstrations and ending. Describe what can be seen; do not infer intent, production source, performance or off-screen events. Mention music or effects only when they help explain an event. Do not inventory every background object or track every movement.

## Spoken words
Transcribe all audible speech verbatim with timestamps and speaker labels when distinguishable. Keep uncertainty visible: use `[inaudible]` or `[unclear: possible words]` at the relevant time. Do not use on-screen captions to fill gaps in speech. Say “No speech heard” only when audio was available; otherwise say audio could not be assessed.

## On-screen text
List readable text in chronological order with timestamps. Preserve its wording. Mark unreadable or partly obscured text instead of guessing. Do not repeat an unchanged overlay at every timestamp.

Keep visual observations, spoken words and written text distinct. Label approximate timestamps as approximate. State any missing coverage or unavailable audio/visual input. Do not add ritual check declarations, psychological explanations, recommendations, or claims that this model output has been independently verified.
