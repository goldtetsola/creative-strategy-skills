# Growthcraft: selected lenses and protocols

#### 2.1.1. EMOTIONAL QUADRANT ENGINEERING (EQE)
**[Pillar I: Attention Architecture]** *[Quick Nav: Fatigue Diagnosis | Creative Refresh | Audience Re-engagement]*

- **Purpose**: Systematically set and rotate the emotional tone of messaging/ad creative to avoid fatigue, trigger new attention, and align with customer state
- **Mechanics**: Uses frameworks (e.g., Circumplex Model of Affect) to classify emotional tone (Valence/Intensity)
- **Use Cases**: Hook refresh, ad fatigue diagnosis, segment-specific persuasion, de-fatiguing messaging
- **Limitations**: Can obscure offer clarity or segmentation needs if over-indexed. Not all emotional wins are replicable
- **AI Application**: Recommends quadrant shift/contrast pivot when decay is detected

**📊 OPERATIONAL DETAILS:**
- **Quadrant Map — Growthcraft valence/arousal** (positive/negative feeling × high/low energy):
  - Growthcraft VA-Q1: High Valence + High Arousal (Excited, Inspired)
  - Growthcraft VA-Q2: Low Valence + High Arousal (Angry, Frustrated)
  - Growthcraft VA-Q3: Low Valence + Low Arousal (Sad, Tired)
  - Growthcraft VA-Q4: High Valence + Low Arousal (Calm, Content)
- **Rotation Strategy**: Try a contrasting feeling/energy combination when it serves the buyer and the test. These labels do not translate to the CCS certainty/emotion map. Save the framework, axes, label and plain emotion with the result.
- **Measurement**: Track CTR/Conv Rate by named Growthcraft valence/arousal treatment; an ad-level difference does not by itself prove the emotion caused it.

**🔗 CONNECTS TO:**
- Ad Autopsy Protocol (§3.1) - for fatigue diagnosis
- Creative Dissection (§3.2) - for understanding emotional mechanics
- Strategic Tempo (§2.1.6) - for timing emotional shifts


#### 2.1.2. STAGE OF AWARENESS LENS (SoA)
**[Pillar II: Belief Engineering]** *[Quick Nav: Message Depth | Offer Sequencing | Nurture Timing]*

- **Purpose**: Locate audience/segment on Problem/Solution/Product/Most-Aware spectrum (five-level awareness model)
- **Use Cases**: Prioritizing message depth/complexity, offer sequencing, nurture vs. harvest timing
- **Limitations**: Audiences can straddle multiple stages; requires input clarity or AI inference
- **AI Application**: Suggests awareness stage and flags friction for mismatch

**Definition owner:** [Additional Lenses — Consumer Awareness](additional-lenses.md#5-levels-of-consumer-awareness) owns the five stages. Its [market-sophistication section](additional-lenses.md#5-levels-of-market-sophistication) owns the separate measure of claim familiarity. Use those definitions rather than a second list here.

**Application at Unaware:** Choose an evidence-supported entry, such as an authority interrupt, category reframe or downstream problem. If the selected hook relies on a **Second-Order Problem**, support the named mechanism and consequence, then connect it truthfully to the product. Other hooks use their own evidence and product connection; they do not need an invented downstream problem. See CCS Stage 3 Non-Moment Development Template. This is an application choice, not another awareness definition.

#### 2.1.3. CUSTOMER IDENTITY ACTIVATION
**[Pillar II: Belief Engineering]** *[Quick Nav: Persona Creation | Tribe Psychology | Aspiration Mapping]*

#### 2.1.4. MESSAGING RATIO BALANCE
**[Pillar III: Value Articulation]** *[Quick Nav: Clarity Testing | Copy Analysis | Message Optimization]*

- **Purpose**: Calibrate the ratio of clarity, detail, intrigue, emotional pull, and functional specificity in messaging
- **Use Cases**: Diagnosing messaging over/under-index, fixing "all sizzle, no steak"
- **Limitations**: Hard to standardize across industries without benchmarks
- **AI Application**: Analyzes copy for over/under-indexing against models

**📊 MESSAGING COMPONENTS:**
- **Clarity**: Explain what is easy or hard to understand, with exact copy evidence. Use a percentage only for a defined comprehension test with its method, numerator, denominator and sample stated.
- **Specificity**: Concrete details vs. abstractions
- **Emotion**: Feeling-based vs. fact-based
- **Intrigue**: Curiosity gaps created
- **Proof**: Evidence/credibility elements

**🔗 CONNECTS TO:**
- Stage of Awareness (§2.1.2) - for stage-appropriate ratios
- Channel-Mechanics (§2.1.9) - for platform-specific optimization
- Creative Dissection (§3.2) - for analyzing winning ratios

#### 2.1.5. PSYCHOLOGICAL FRICTION MAPPING
**[Pillar II: Belief Engineering]** *[Quick Nav: Friction Points | Objection Handling | Trust Building]*

- **Purpose**: Map the exact point in the customer journey where belief collapses, risk spikes, or perceived effort outweighs value
- **Core Sub-Frameworks**: Value vs. Effort, Credibility vs. Trust, Risk vs. Reward
- **Use Cases**: Bottom-of-funnel conversion decay, onboarding drop-off, risk reversal copy
- **AI Application**: Identifies metrics/phrases that suggest friction, proposes friction-removal tactics

**📊 FRICTION TYPES & SOLUTIONS:**
- **Value vs. Effort**: 
  - Friction: "Is this worth my time/money?"
  - Solution: Simplify process, emphasize ROI, quick wins
- **Credibility vs. Trust**:
  - Friction: "Can I believe these claims?"
  - Solution: Social proof, credentials, third-party validation
- **Risk vs. Reward**:
  - Friction: "What if I'm disappointed?"
  - Solution: Guarantees, trials, reversibility

**🔗 CONNECTS TO:**
- Funnel Analysis Tools - for identifying drop-off points
- Ad Autopsy (§3.1) - when friction causes performance decay
- Unit-Economics (§2.1.8) - for viable friction-removal strategies

### 3.6. Persuasion Architecture (Revelation Engineering)
**[Connected to: Pillar II - Belief Engineering]**

**Purpose**: Systematically engineer prospect's journey to buying realization

**Key Insight**: Revelation isn't random; it's engineered through systematic belief transformation

#### REVELATION ARCHITECTURE™ COMPONENTS:

**1. Current State Diagnosis**
- Where is prospect now? (Awareness stage)
- What do they currently believe?
- What's blocking progress?

**2. Desired State Mapping**
- What must they believe to buy?
- What realization creates urgency?
- What makes decision feel inevitable?

**3. Belief Gap Analysis**
- List all belief transitions needed
- Prioritize by impact and difficulty
- Identify evidence for each shift

**4. Revelation Sequence Design**
- Order belief shifts logically
- Build momentum through progression
- Create "aha" moments strategically

**5. Environmental Engineering**
- Remove contradicting signals
- Amplify supporting evidence
- Control narrative flow

#### IMPLEMENTATION TACTICS:
- **Micro-commitments**: Small yeses leading to big yes
- **Proof Stacking**: Multiple evidence types
- **Objection Preemption**: Address before they arise
- **Future Pacing**: Help them see life after purchase

**🔗 CONNECTS TO:**
- Stage of Awareness (§2.1.2) - starting point for journey
- Psychological Friction (§2.1.5) - barriers to remove
- Customer Identity (§2.1.3) - transformation they seek
